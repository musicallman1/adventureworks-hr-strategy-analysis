# AdventureWorks HR & Sales Strategy Analysis

An executive-facing analysis of AdventureWorks' hiring, sales, and workforce diversity trends, built for a newly formed leadership team following a company merger. Combines a Tableau dashboard with a data-storytelling presentation translating the findings into specific, time-bound recommendations.

## The story

Hiring at AdventureWorks has plateaued, and with a new executive team in place post-merger, there's a clear need to reassess strategy. This analysis looks at four levers to address it:

1. **Framing strategy by order type** — offline and online sales both drive meaningful revenue, but the right growth strategy differs by market (e.g. a European/UK strategy for offline sales, cross-channel funnel support in Australia and Canada).
2. **Stock Turnover Ratio (STR) as a key metric** — using STR to guide both sales and hiring strategy.
3. **Reorganizing Sales, Marketing, & Information Services** — online sales growth supports integrating these departments, with new hires filling integrated sales-marketing-technology roles rather than legacy structures.
4. **Leveraging diversity for impact** — women are the top three salespeople by STR, revealing a clear opportunity (and a pay-equity gap to close) in strategic hiring.

## Recommendations

- Increase new hires within 2 years by **+25**
- Grow the share of women employees from **27% to 32%**
- Integrate Sales, Marketing, and Information Services around online sales growth
- Additional focus areas: a European offline market strategy, Australia/Canada channel-specific sales support, pay equity for women in Sales, and hiring accessibility for other demographics

## Background

Built as part of a data-storytelling exercise: given a prior sprint's HR/sales dashboard, translate the underlying analysis into a ~15-25 minute presentation for a senior, strategic audience with no prior context on the business (simulating a post-merger executive team). Structured around a Situation-Complication-Resolution narrative, with SMART-framed recommendations.
